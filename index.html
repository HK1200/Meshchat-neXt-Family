neXt Family Privacy Policy

neXt Family is developed by HK1200. For privacy questions, contact hk1200.dev@gmail.com.

Family stores approved contacts, room configuration, message history and settings on the device. A guardian sets a local password and decides which people, rooms and services are available. SIP and room credentials use the device’s protected storage. If you send a message, photo, file or call, Family transmits what that feature needs over the configured network to the contact, room, radio, relay or SIP provider involved. Other room participants can see messages posted to that room. A display name and network address can be advertised through a configured Reticulum network.

A guardian can enable location sharing. When active, Family sends location updates to approved contacts using a compatible service. Family uses system location permission for features including regional map selection and location sharing. Map download services and configured connection providers may receive network metadata such as your IP address. Family uses the camera to scan QR codes, the microphone for calls, and Bluetooth for compatible radios.

Local data remains on the device until it is deleted through an available app control or by clearing the app’s local storage. Clearing storage removes locally stored settings and identities. Recipients and external services may retain copies they received. Guardians can manage the device's sharing settings, permissions and approved contacts or rooms.

Optional mailbox alerts

Family does not choose or enable an alert provider automatically. In compatible push-enabled iOS builds, a guardian can choose an HTTPS provider and enable mailbox alerts. Personal-signed development previews cannot receive live Apple push alerts.

The chosen provider receives the Apple push token, the device’s public Reticulum address and approved senders’ public keys. A private enrollment code authenticates registration updates. After a configured relay accepts encrypted mail, a configured sender can submit a signed hint containing public routing addresses, an encrypted-message identifier, a timestamp and signature. Alert requests do not contain message text, file/photo contents or location coordinates. Apple receives the push token and a generic alert without a contact name or message content. Connection operators may also see network metadata.

Turning alerts off completes after the provider acknowledges removal; offline removal remains pending until it can be retried. The current reference provider removes active registration data on acknowledgment and expires delivery metadata after about one hour while running. Other providers and operator-managed logs, configuration and backups may have different retention. Turning alerts off does not delete mail held by a relay or copies received by another person.

Rooms and Relay Chat are excluded from Google Play and planned App Store editions. GitHub editions may include those guardian-controlled features.
