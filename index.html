neXt Family Privacy Policy

neXt Family is developed by HK1200. For privacy questions, contact hk1200.dev@gmail.com.

Family stores approved contacts, room configuration, message history and settings on the device. A guardian sets a local password and decides which people, rooms and services are available. SIP and room credentials use Android protected storage. If you send a message, photo, file or call, Family transmits what that feature needs over the configured network to the contact, room, radio, relay or SIP provider involved. Other room participants can see messages posted to that room. A display name and network address can be advertised through a configured Reticulum network.

A guardian can enable location sharing. When active, Family sends location updates to approved contacts using a compatible service. Family uses Android location permission for features including regional map selection and location sharing. Map download services and configured connection providers may receive network metadata such as your IP address. Family uses the camera to scan QR codes, the microphone for calls, and Bluetooth for compatible radios.

Local data remains on the device until it is deleted through an available app control or by clearing the app's Android storage. Clearing storage removes locally stored settings and identities. Recipients and external services may retain copies they received. Guardians can manage the device's sharing settings, permissions and approved contacts or rooms.
